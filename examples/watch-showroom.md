# Example: Luxury Watch Showroom

## Goal

Build a premium 3D watch experience that emphasizes dial detail, case finish, strap material and proportions.

## Visual direction

- Dark, neutral or warm luxury studio chosen to fit the brand.
- Fewer simultaneously visible products than the sneaker grid.
- Detailed hero presentation with optional product carousel.
- Controlled reflections and low bloom to preserve dial markings.
- Close-up camera framing based on actual watch bounds.

## Interactions

- Select a watch for detailed inspection.
- Rotate or orbit in a controlled range if requested.
- Switch dial, strap or case finish when supported by real product variants.
- Display actual dimensions and material information.
- Keep variant selection and product data available in HTML.

## Fallback and validation

Use a product image or simplified watch model if the GLB is absent. Validate that numerals, hands, materials and fine details remain readable at target screen sizes. Avoid invented specifications or availability.
