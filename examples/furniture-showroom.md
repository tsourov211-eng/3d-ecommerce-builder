# Example: Furniture and Interior Showroom

## Goal

Present furniture at believable scale and, when requested, inside a contextual room scene.

## Visual direction

- Room vignette or staged product zones instead of a tiny 2x5 pedestal grid.
- Correct ground contact and approximate real-world dimensions.
- Lighting that shows fabric, timber, metal and surface finish accurately.
- Optional room context, wall and floor materials.

## Interactions

- Select a furniture item to inspect it.
- Orbit or reposition the camera without crossing through geometry.
- Change fabric, color or finish only where catalog variants exist.
- Show dimensions and material information in semantic HTML.
- Support a static product image fallback on mobile or unsupported devices.

## Validation

Check model units, pivot position, scale, wall / floor collision, mobile camera framing and product variant accuracy. Do not display fabricated dimensions.
